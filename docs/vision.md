# Project NGX

## Vision

Project NGX is a preorder management system designed to help small business owners organize every stage of the preorder process.

The goal is to replace manual record keeping with a simple, reliable, and modern application that manages:

- Customers
- Products
- Orders
- Payments
- Shipping batches
- Delivery tracking
- Business reports

Project NGX exists to reduce stress, save time, and help preorder businesses grow with confidence.

---

## Core Principles

1. Simple enough for anyone to use.
2. Fast enough to save time every day.
3. Reliable enough that no order is forgotten.
4. Beautiful enough that using it feels enjoyable.
5. Built with real business needs in mind.
