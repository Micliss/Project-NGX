Mum identifies product
       ↓
Contacts supplier in China
       ↓
Supplier confirms availability
       ↓
Supplier provides product information/price
       ↓
Mum decides to offer product
this is the first way

Supplier sends available products
       ↓
Mum reviews products
       ↓
Mum selects products she wants to offer
this is the second way
A product should have things like:

Product Code
Product Name
Category
Supplier
Supplier Cost
Customer Price
MOQ
Shipping Method
Availability
